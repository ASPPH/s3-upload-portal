Share a PDF on the web

Upload a PDF and get a permanent public link you can use on aspph.org, in emails and in newsletters.

Everything you upload is public. Anyone with the link can open it. Don't upload confidential files or anything with personal information (PII).

Before you upload

  • PDF files only, up to 50 MB.
  • Give it a name people will recognize, like 2025-annual-report.pdf rather than final.pdf.
  • Leave out labels like "final", "new" or "v3". They go out of date fast.
  • Don't worry about spaces or capitals. We tidy the name for you and show the final link before you upload.

Updating a file?

Upload the new version with the same filename. The link stays the same, so everywhere it's already shared shows the new file.
